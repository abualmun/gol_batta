SyncVid

Watch together, from two places, at the same moment.

You and a friend each open the same video file on your own device. When either of you plays, pauses or skips, the other follows. The video is never uploaded.

Your friend sees this in the chat.

How it works

  1. Create a room and send the link to one friend.
  2. Each of you opens the same video file from your own device.
  3. Press play. Pauses, skips and chat are shared.

Works best with MP4 files. Rooms hold two people.
